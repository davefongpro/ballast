Privacy

Ballast, a tool from Newton’s First Labs. Last updated 23 August 2026.

Your work never leaves your browser. We count page views so we know the tool is being used, and that is all.

What stays on your device

Everything you type. Your ideas, their names and descriptions, every score, every measure you define, your benchmarks, your notes, your priorities and categories. All of it lives in your browser’s local storage so your work survives a reload. None of it is transmitted anywhere, and there is no server that could receive it if it were.

Pasting and importing happen entirely inside the page. Exporting a CSV writes a file straight from your browser. Clearing your browser’s site data for this site erases everything, and nobody keeps a copy.

What is counted

We use Vercel Web Analytics, which records:

  • That a page was viewed, and when
  • Which site or link you arrived from
  • Country, browser and whether you are on a phone or a computer

It does not use cookies, does not track you across other websites, and does not build a profile of you. See Vercel’s analytics privacy policy for their side of it.

What is never counted

The contents of your work. No idea name, description, note, score, measure or category is ever recorded, transmitted or seen by anyone but you. This is not a policy we could change quietly: the app has no code that sends your data anywhere, and the source is public so you can check.

No accounts

There is no sign-up, no login and no email address to give. We have no way of knowing who you are.
